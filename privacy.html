Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialUploader ("we", "our", or "us") is a macOS desktop application that helps creators upload and publish videos to social media platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth tokens from social media platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X/Twitter, Bluesky) that you explicitly authorize. These are stored securely in your macOS Keychain.
  • Video Content: Videos you choose to upload are temporarily stored in cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) solely for the purpose of distributing them to your selected platforms. They are deleted immediately after publishing.
  • Profile Information: Profile names and platform configurations you create within the app, stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate with social media platforms on your behalf
  • To upload and publish your video content to platforms you select
  • To save your preferences and credentials locally for convenience

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain, Apple's secure credential storage system
  • Videos are temporarily uploaded to Cloudflare R2 and deleted automatically after publishing
  • No data is sent to any server owned or operated by us
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage data

5. Third-Party Services

We interact with the following third-party services based on your selections:

  • TikTok (via Content Posting API)
  • YouTube (via YouTube Data API v3)
  • Facebook / Instagram (via Graph API)
  • X / Twitter
  • Bluesky (via AT Protocol)
  • Cloudflare R2 (temporary video storage)

Each platform has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention

We do not retain any of your data on our servers. All data is stored locally on your device or in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket. You can delete all stored credentials at any time by removing your profile in the app.

7. Children's Privacy

SocialUploader is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
